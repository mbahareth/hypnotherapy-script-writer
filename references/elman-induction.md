# Elman Induction

Dave Elman rapid path: compliance → eyelid catalepsy → body spread → fractionation → number fade. Then change work. Do not stack a second long induction after the numbers are gone.

## When

User said Elman, rapid, somnambulism, eye catalepsy, lose the numbers. Fast first-session depth. Live 1:1 with a willing adult.

Skip or soften for sleep files, gentle/anxiety-first, control-sensitive wording, group rooms, recordings where no one can see a failed test.

## Live 1:1 (4–7 min)

Arm drop is live only.

[SET] One long breath. Eyes can close. You can hear this voice. You can feel the chair.

[LIDS] Attention to the eyelids. Relax those muscles until they will not work. When you are sure — test them. (pause 4s) If they open: that was testing that they work. Relax them past working. Test again. Good. Stop testing. Send that ease brow to feet. (pause 5s)

[FRACTIONATION] Eyes open for a breath, then close twice as deep. One two three open. (pause 2s) And close. Twice as deep. (pause 3s) Open… and close. Twice again. (pause 4s) Two to four cycles.

[ARM live] Lift the hand by the thumb and let it drop. When it drops the body can follow that limp.

[NUMBERS] Count out loud from one hundred. Each number doubles the mental ease. By ninety-eight the numbers can be gone. If a number is still there, let it fall out on the next breath.

[CHANGE WORK] Start suggestions now.

## Recorded audio (3–5 min)

No try-to-open-and-fail as the only move. No arm drop. Listener does not have to speak.

Lids: no need to open them. If a test-thought arises, let it pass. The lids stay.

Fractionation: a part opens toward the room for one breath and closes twice as heavy. A millimeter of light and gone, twice as deep.

Numbers: voice counts one hundred, ninety-nine, ninety-eight… and gone.

## Failed tests

Eyes fly open → one retry, then no-need language. Giggle → that shake can melt on the next close. Count past 90 → stop the count. More alert after open/close → slower close, fewer cycles. Control-sensitive → never you-cannot-open. Sleep brief → do not use Elman as the main road.

Never argue with a failed test.

## After Elman

Optional short staircase on 20+ min files. Direct triad + future pace. Emergence 1–5. Analog marks begin in change work, not during the lid test.

Do not open with you-are-getting-sleepy. Do not run six deepeners after numbers are gone. Do not put live arm-drop language on a phone track. Fus'ha if Arabic.
