# Milton Model Presuppositions

A proposition the sentence treats as already true. Deny the main clause and it still sits in the background.
Park given material in adjuncts and triggers. Mark the act, not the trigger.
Spoken scripts do not say presupposition.

Two triggers per sentence. Three in a stack line. Not five.

Existence: the N, that N, your N.
Relative: the N that VP.
Time: when, after, before, as, while, during, until.
Change of state: already, still, anymore, begin, stop, continue.
Awareness / factive: notice, realize, aware, know, see that.
Ordinal / additive: again, another, more, even, back.
Or-bind: this breath or the next — both roads presuppose arrival.

PHS: when + already.
Sleep: as / while only. No tags.
Do not hang the act only under said / dreamed / maybe.
