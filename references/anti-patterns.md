# Anti-Patterns

Kill these before delivery.

- Relax used as a drum
- You are getting sleepy as line one
- Try to
- Don't think of the old habit (as the only move)
- Ten virtues in one sentence
- Medical lecture inside trance
- Baby talk
- Change work with no depth cue first
- Future pace only in five years, never today
- No silence after a triad
- More than seven analog marks in twenty minutes
- Chain of four post-hypnotics
- Naming swish, submodalities, or dissociation in the spoken script
