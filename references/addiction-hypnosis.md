# Addiction and Craving Hypnosis

Urge work for a named habit. Regulation, replacement, wave falls. No miracle-cure claim.

End-state: urge rises and falls; hands already on the replacement; old habit is an alarm that can stand down; identity after the two-second act.

Spine: progressive or Elman-blend → breath fractionation → shrink-and-park the reach-picture → PHS on the physical cue → future pace today + old trigger → AWAKE unless they asked sleep.

1. Name reach, lift, use.
2. Stop at beat two. Insert /leave it there/.
3. Old picture smaller/grey; new hands own-eyes.
4. Same cue in future pace and PHS.

Anti-patterns: never want X again; shame as deepener; ten substances; sleep ending on a commute file.
