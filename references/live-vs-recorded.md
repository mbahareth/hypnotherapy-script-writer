# Live vs Recorded

## Recorded audio (default)

- No questions that need a spoken answer
- Rossi pauses stay silent
- Utilization names generic room facts
- Emergence extra crisp
- Short loop is a separate block

## Live 1:1

- Ask two questions max, then utilize the answer
- Fractionation is stronger because you can see the eyelids
- If they cough, swallow, or smile, say it and lead
- Keep a one-line emergency emergence
- Do not read analog marks out loud as slash marks

## Group live

- Double binds and choice language
- No single-person diagnosis
- Shorter silences (4–6s)
- Count emergence together
