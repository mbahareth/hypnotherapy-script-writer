# Fractionation

Leave a lighter state and re-enter a deeper one. Each re-entry uses the last depth as the floor. Deepener and convincer, not an induction by itself and not change work.

Dose: two cycles in a 20-minute file. Three to six only live when you can see the face.

## Recipes

1. Eyelid open/close (Elman core). Recorded rewrite as imagined millimeter.
2. Count-up / count-down. Partial emergence then past the old floor. Once or twice.
3. Alert-and-double. No eyes required. A little more alert… and twice as deep. Two cycles max in twenty minutes.
4. Breath pulse. This inhale a little more awake. This exhale twice as heavy. Never hold the breath as the trick. Sleep-adjacent default.
5. Attention in/out. One sound in the room, then the space behind the sound. Outside… and inside, twice as quiet. No spoken answers on a recording.
6. Stair up then deeper down. Long files only.
7. Live snap. Audio: no.

Pick one recipe per session unless the file is 30+ minutes.

## Dose

8–12 min: one recipe, two cycles after induction.
18–22: end of induction or start of deepener, two cycles.
30–45: after induction and once before identity, two recipes max.
Sleep: breath pulse or skip. Do not pop the eyes open.
Live Elman: after lids, before numbers, two to four open/close.

Never fractionate after the last future-pace. Emergence is one-way.

## Ugly cycles

They look brighter and stay → you emerged them. Close slower. Drop to breath pulse.
Giggle → utilize one line, one clean close.
Dizziness → both feet, shorter cycles, no confusion on top.
Control-sensitive → offer the close as the comfortable option.
Already deep → skip.

Mark pause after every open and every close. Voice climbs on up, drops on down. Do not say fractionation on the track. Arabic: تفتح / تغلق / أعمق مرتين.
