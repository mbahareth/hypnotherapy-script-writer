# Sleep, Awake, and Ending Types

Every script names one ending: AWAKE | SLEEP-ONSET | SLEEP-AFTER | CONTINUE-SLEEPING.

## Awake (default)
Emergence 1–5. Body, room, time, eyes, stand.

## Sleep-onset
The session is the road to sleep. No eye-opening fractionation. No alert-and-oriented. Longer pauses, heavier K. Permission to not finish the recording.

## Sleep-after
Full change work first, then permission to stay down if this is night.

## Continue-sleeping
Already in bed. No pre-talk that wakes them. Deepener only. Leave them there.

## Awake hypnosis
Eyes soft or open. No sleep language. Light emergence.
